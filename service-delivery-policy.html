No physical goods

Vailongara Kalari provides services only. We do not sell or ship physical products, and prasadam is not sent by post.

How services are delivered

  • Booking confirmation is sent on WhatsApp immediately after successful payment (or immediately, for cash-at-office bookings).
  • Direct consultations take place at the Kalari on the booked date, between 10 AM and 4 PM, in order of arrival.
  • Online consultations are delivered as a phone call from the Acharya to the number given at booking, during the booked 30-minute window between 7 PM and 10 PM.
  • Poojas are performed at the Kshetram on the booked date (Vishesha poojas 10 AM – 2 PM; Amavasi Kalasha Pooja 11 AM – 1:30 PM).
